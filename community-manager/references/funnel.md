# Full-funnel content strategy

Every post does one job. A post trying to do all four does none, and that is what most
brand accounts sound like.

## Awareness

**Who**: people who do not know you exist.
**Goal**: reach, and being worth a follow.
**Measure**: impressions, reach, profile visits, follower growth.

Content that works: a position on something your audience argues about, the frustration
they have not seen named out loud, humor that only makes sense if you do their job,
genuinely useful things with no ask attached.

**CTA**: soft or none. Follow, save, share. A conversion CTA here reads as an ad from a
stranger, and it is why most awareness content underperforms.

The trap is being interesting about nothing. Awareness content still has to connect to the
problem you solve, or you build an audience for your jokes rather than your product.

## Consideration

**Who**: they have seen you, they are deciding whether you are worth their attention.
**Goal**: trust, and understanding what the thing actually does.
**Measure**: saves, shares, comments, DMs, profile clicks, signups.

Content that works: how it works, shown rather than described. Customer stories with real
details. Educational content adjacent to the problem. Comparisons that are honest about
what you are not for, which build more trust than any claim about what you are.

**CTA**: low friction and specific. Name what happens next and how long it takes.

## Conversion

**Who**: they understand the value, they have not acted.
**Goal**: the one action that matters for your model, defined in your brand profile.
**Measure**: that action.

Content that works: demonstrations of outcome rather than feature. Objection handling, out
loud, including the objection you would rather not mention. Specific results, with real
numbers only.

**CTA**: direct, one, unmissable. This is the one stage where being explicit is correct.

If your model has a paid tier, **show the value and let people ask**. A screenshot of what
the paid surface does out-converts a feature table, because it triggers a question instead
of a defence. Keep upgrade content to at most one post in ten.

## Retention and advocacy

**Who**: already using it.
**Goal**: keep them active, and make them want to tell someone.
**Measure**: return visits, repeat actions, referrals, organic mentions.

Content that works: spotlighting users by name and with permission, celebrating their
milestones rather than yours, community wins, teaching the deeper parts of the product to
people who have earned them.

**CTA**: participation and identity. Share yours, tag someone, post an update.

This is the most under-invested stage and the cheapest growth available. A user who feels
seen brings people the ad budget cannot.

## Weekly mix

Default 40 awareness / 30 consideration / 20 conversion / 10 retention. Adjust for stage:

- **Pre-launch**: heavier awareness and consideration, near-zero conversion.
- **Growing**: the default.
- **Monetizing an existing base**: shift toward conversion and retention, but never past
  the 10:1 value-to-pitch ratio.

## Never post

Generic motivational quotes. Engagement bait with no substance. Fabricated metrics or
stories. Fake urgency and invented scarcity. Paid promotion without disclosure. Pricing
before value. Competitor attacks by name. Anything you would be embarrassed to have quoted
back to you in six months.
